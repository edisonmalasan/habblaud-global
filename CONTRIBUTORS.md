## Why "claude" appears in the contributors graph

Most commits in the original Habblaud history carry a `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer. GitHub counts co-authors in its contributors graph, so `claude` is credited alongside the human authors — in the original repository too, where it is also the largest single entry (218). No commit author was changed in this repository; the graph simply reflects those original trailers.
